## What that establishes

1. **The product is a crypto trading bot.**

2. **The blueprint comes before the bot.** The diagram is the current deliverable,
   not a sketch on the way to code. Energy goes into perfecting the plan first,
   the way an architect perfects a skyscraper's drawings before anything is poured.

3. **Every feature is a replaceable spare part.** Any part can be swapped for a
   better version, and new parts can be added, without the rest of the system
   being rewritten.

4. **The data flow must not degrade as parts are added.** However many parts go
   in, the flow between them stays consistent and legible. This is the hard
   constraint the whole architecture is judged against — it is what the diagram
   has to prove, not merely assert.

5. **The diagram's focus is communication between features**, not the internals
   of any one feature.

6. **The user cannot open files on this server.** Every artefact meant to be seen
   must be published as a browser link. This is standing, not per-request.

7. **The dashboard must stay true to what was discussed and to what is actually
   on the server.** No aspirational tiles, no invented state.
